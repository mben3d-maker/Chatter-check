How to use it
  1. Pick the tool and enter the RPM, flutes and feed that are running.
  2. Optional: with the spindle running at that RPM but not cutting, tap Listen, then Save baseline. Peaks that show up there get tagged as machine noise.
  3. Tap Reset hold, then listen while it chatters. Peak hold keeps the highest level at each frequency, so short bursts still show.
  4. The tallest peak tagged CHATTER is the one to fix. Tap it.
  5. A peak on a whole multiple of tooth-pass is each flute hitting (normal, or forced vibration if it's loud). Changing RPM doesn't fix forced vibration; stiffen the setup or lighten the cut.
  6. Try the suggested RPM with the spindle override, then save the reading with the result.

Phone mics are good to about 15–20 kHz; most chatter is between 500 and 5,000 Hz. Keep the phone out of the coolant and close to the window or door.
