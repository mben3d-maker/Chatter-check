CHATTER CHECK - QUICK HOW-TO
============================
Finds chatter by sound and tells you which spindle speed (RPM) to try.
Works on any phone, tablet or PC with Chrome and a microphone.

OPEN IT
  Chrome -> https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/chatter/chatter_check.html
  Save it: Chrome menu (3 dots) -> Add to Home screen.
  First time you tap Listen: choose ALLOW for the microphone.

ONE-TIME SETUP (Tools tab)
  Add each tool: name, flutes, usual RPM, feed (IPM),
  and the MACHINE's max and min RPM.
  Tools and readings save on this phone only.

AT THE MACHINE
  1. Listen tab -> pick the tool in "Cut". Check the RPM, flutes and feed.
     Use the RPM that is ACTUALLY running (include spindle override).
  2. Baseline (optional but recommended):
     Spindle running at cut RPM, NOT cutting -> Listen -> Save baseline.
  3. Tap Reset hold right before the tool enters the cut.
  4. Listen during the cut. Hold the phone near the door or window,
     out of the coolant.
  5. Tap Stop or Freeze when you have a good reading.

READING THE GRAPH
  Range buttons (2k/5k/10k/20k) only zoom the graph. Start on 5 kHz.
  Blue dashed lines = tooth-pass (each flute hitting). Normal.

  Peak labels:
    CHATTER          -> the problem. Tap it.
    TOOTH-PASS xN    -> flutes hitting. Normal.
    SPINDLE xN       -> spindle runout / balance.
    MACHINE NOISE    -> also in the baseline. Ignore.

FIXING IT (Chatter fix box)
  Tapping a CHATTER peak fills in the frequency.
  Try the "First" RPM using spindle override.
  The feed shown keeps the same chip load.
  Still a smaller chatter peak? Try the next RPM in the list.

  "FORCED VIBRATION" means changing RPM won't help.
  Stiffen the setup, shorten the tool or holder, or lighten the cut.

  Then: add a note, pick the Result -> Save reading to log.

LOG TAB
  All saved readings. Download CSV or Copy as text to email them.

TIPS
  - Most chatter is between 500 and 5,000 Hz. Long tools run lower.
  - Change RPM before you slow the feed. Slowing the feed alone
    often doesn't stop chatter.
  - Put the RPM that worked into the program, and save it as a
    proven cut in CNC Shop Calculator.
  - Demo button = sample reading, for practice.
